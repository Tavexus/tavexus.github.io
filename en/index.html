Why Tavexus?

Your data stays with you

The program runs on your own computer. Your customer, sales and document data are not sent to any cloud.

Works even when the internet is down

Your daily work doesn't depend on an internet connection. Phones and tablets on the same network can connect too.

Automatic backups and updates

An automatic backup every night, plus an optional second copy on a USB drive. New features and fixes are installed automatically overnight.

Built with KVKK in mind

Each program was written for a specific type of business, so its screens, documents and calculations speak the language of that trade.

How we work

  1. Take a look

    Open the demo, watch the video or ask us on WhatsApp. If you like, we can show you the program at your workplace.

  2. Try it free

    We install the program on your computer, and you use it with your own data during the free trial period.

  3. Rent it

    If you like it, you continue with a one-time installation fee and a monthly, quarterly or yearly rental.

  4. Relax

    Support, bug fixes and updates are included for as long as you rent. If a payment is missed, your data is not deleted.

Payment: by bank transfer (havale / EFT), with an invoice. We agree on the price and the installation date together — just send us a message for a quote.
